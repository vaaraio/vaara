Receipts

Every decision leaves a signed receipt, chained to the one before it and stamped by a clock you do not control. Anyone can check it on their own machine, without Vaara and without a key.

Proof that holds up without trusting the one who made it.

One per decision

What was asked, what was decided, why, and what happened after.

Chained

Removing or changing one receipt breaks the chain, so a gap shows.

Stamped by an outside clock

A trusted timestamp, including EU-qualified ones, shows the receipt existed at that time.

Checked offline

A standalone checker reads the receipt alone: no Vaara, no network, no key.
