The gate

Vaara's gate sits in front of the systems software can reach. Every action is scored against your rules: allowed, held for a person, or refused. A session whose risk keeps adding up gets less room to act until a person approves again.

Every action is checked before it happens.

Your rules, in a file

A policy file says what is allowed, what needs a person and what is never allowed. The same file works on every surface.

A person where it matters

Held actions wait for a yes or a no. The answer is recorded with who gave it.

Room that shrinks with risk

Risky actions in one session add up. Below a set level, even ordinary actions wait for a person until someone approves. New in 2.8.

Fails closed

If the gate cannot decide, the action does not run.
