Look up whether a digest was published to a public transparency log, and when. Publishing is opt-in and off by default, so an absence here means nothing was published, not that nothing happened.

Before you opt in: a published head is public to everyone, permanently. Anyone can see that a digest was published and when, and that includes us. It is the only usage signal Vaara ever receives, and it exists because you switched it on. A digest reveals no content, no size and no identity, but the timing is visible to anybody watching the log.

A passing signature proves the payload is byte-identical to what the holder of that key signed. It says nothing about whether the key belongs to who you think, or whether the statement is true. Pin the public key from a source the signer does not control before treating a pass as meaningful.
