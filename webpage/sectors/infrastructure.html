Energy, industry and infrastructure

Operators are adding AI assistants and automation to maintenance, dispatch and data-centre operations. Vaara sits in front of the actions those systems can take, holds the risky ones for an operator, and keeps a record the regulator and the insurer can read.

When software can change a setpoint, open a valve or shed load, the change needs a gate and a record.

What you can do

  • Let automation handle routine operations while risky changes wait for an operator.
  • Keep one record across vendors and control systems.
  • Show a regulator or an insurer what was changed, by what, and under whose approval.

What you are saved from

  • Changes that nobody can attribute after an outage.
  • Evidence split across every vendor's own logging.
  • An AI assistant acting on a system it was never meant to reach.

What it costs

Free to download and use, with nothing to sign up for. Paid work, such as a readiness review or a pilot in your own environment, is scoped to your case and priced as a fixed amount agreed before it starts. No subscription, no per-seat fee. Write to hello@vaara.io.

What it saves you

  • Root-cause time after incidents.
  • Duplicate evidence work for each regulator and insurer.
  • Downtime from changes that should have waited for a person.

Every action ends in one of three states, and leaves a receipt

✓

Allowed

Inside the rules: it goes through.

‖

Needs a person

Unusual or risky: it waits until a person says yes or no.

✕

Refused

Against the rules: it never happens.

▣

Receipt

Every one of the three leaves a signed record of what was decided and why.
