Healthcare

Decision support, triage tools and documentation assistants act inside clinical work. Vaara checks each automated step against your rules, routes the ones that need a clinician to a clinician, and keeps a record of who approved what, before it reached the patient record.

In healthcare the question is not only what the system suggested, but who approved it and when.

What you can do

  • Use decision support and documentation assistants with the approval step on record.
  • Show that a clinician approved an entry before it reached the patient record.
  • Keep evidence that a hospital, a supplier and a supervisor can all read the same way.

What you are saved from

  • An approval claim that rests on screenshots and goodwill.
  • Supplier logs that you cannot read without the supplier.
  • Rebuilding a timeline by hand after an incident.

What it costs

Free to download and use, with nothing to sign up for. Paid work, such as a readiness review or a pilot in your own environment, is scoped to your case and priced as a fixed amount agreed before it starts. No subscription, no per-seat fee. Write to hello@vaara.io.

What it saves you

  • Clinician and quality-team time per incident review.
  • A separate audit project for every new AI tool.
  • Delay in rollouts that wait on the evidence question.

Every action ends in one of three states, and leaves a receipt

✓

Allowed

Inside the rules: it goes through.

‖

Needs a person

Unusual or risky: it waits until a person says yes or no.

✕

Refused

Against the rules: it never happens.

▣

Receipt

Every one of the three leaves a signed record of what was decided and why.
