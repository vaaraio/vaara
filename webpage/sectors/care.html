Care and social services

Care homes and home care increasingly let systems decide: a door that locks for the night, a medication reminder, an alarm that escalates or does not, a care note drafted by software. Vaara checks each of those against your rules and keeps a receipt a family or an inspector can check.

When software acts on people who cannot question it, the record has to hold up for them.

What you can do

  • Automate night locking, alarms and documentation without losing the right to say who decided what.
  • Let a nurse approve the unusual cases in the moment, and let the routine ones run.
  • Answer a family's question about one resident on one night in minutes, with the record itself.

What you are saved from

  • Having only your own logs to show when a family, an inspector or a court asks.
  • Automation that quietly drifts outside your self-monitoring plan.
  • Arguments about whether a note was approved before it reached the record system.

What it costs

Free to download and use, with nothing to sign up for. Paid work, such as a readiness review or a pilot in your own environment, is scoped to your case and priced as a fixed amount agreed before it starts. No subscription, no per-seat fee. Write to hello@vaara.io.

What it saves you

  • Hours of reconstruction for every complaint or inspection question.
  • A rollout that would otherwise stall at the first supervisory question.
  • The cost of re-auditing automation each time a supplier changes it.

Every action ends in one of three states, and leaves a receipt

✓

Allowed

Inside the rules: it goes through.

‖

Needs a person

Unusual or risky: it waits until a person says yes or no.

✕

Refused

Against the rules: it never happens.

▣

Receipt

Every one of the three leaves a signed record of what was decided and why.
