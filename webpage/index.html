What Vaara does

Checked before it happens

Every action passes the gate first: allowed, held for a person, or refused. Nothing slips through for being fast.

A person where it matters

Unusual actions wait for a yes or a no on your desktop. The answer is recorded with who gave it.

Proof that travels

Each decision leaves a signed receipt. Years later, offline, anyone can check it is genuine and unchanged.

The wall of your choice

Run agents in the sandbox you trust. Twelve are supported, and every receipt says which one was in use.

Ready for the questions you will be asked

Records are mapped to the EU AI Act, DORA, GDPR and SOC 2 controls, in one package an inspector can read.

Stays in your house

Runs on your own machines. No account, no telemetry, nothing sent to us. The code is open to your own people.

Software that acts on its own, inside your rules. And proof of every action that anyone can check.

Things already happen with no person in the moment

A payment goes out. Access is granted. A door locks for the night. A file is deleted. More and more of these are decided by software, and AI agents are one example of it.

Later someone asks what happened and why. Most systems answer with their own logs, which means asking to be taken at your word. Vaara answers with a receipt that holds up without trusting you, or us.

Every action ends in one of three states, and leaves a receipt

✓

Allowed

Inside the rules: it goes through.

‖

Needs a person

Unusual or risky: it waits until a person says yes or no.

✕

Refused

Against the rules: it never happens.

▣

Receipt

Every one of the three leaves a signed record of what was decided and why.

A receipt that outlasts the system that made it

Years later, on a plane with the wifi off, without Vaara installed and without trusting you or us, anyone can check that a receipt is genuine, when it was made and that nobody has changed it since.
